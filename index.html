02. Information We Collect

Locally stored information

Game progress, earned stars, best scores, unlocked dealers, sound and vibration preferences and gameplay settings are stored on your device to maintain your gameplay experience.

Automatically collected information

No name, telephone number, email address or payment details are required to play. If you contact us, we may process your email address and information you voluntarily provide.
